# 🔥 Study Watch

A cozy little campfire to study beside.

A glass timer hangs over a rainy, low-poly night camp — pick **10 / 30 / 45 / 60** minutes, hit Start, and get to work while three tiny friends keep the fire company.

![Study Watch](screenshots/readme-hero.png)

## The friends

| | Name | Species | Signature moves |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 🟠 | **Miso** | Red panda | Waddles around, rears up on his hind legs to sniff the air |
| ⚪ | **Mochi** | Cat | Glides low, grooms herself, butt-wiggle → **pounce** |
| 🟡 | **Hachi** | Inu | Bouncy trot, rain shake-offs, random **zoomies** |

They wander wherever they like, nap by the flames (Zzz…), and perk up when you start a session.

![Camp life](screenshots/readme-camp.png)

## Things to try

- **Click a friend** to pet them — hearts! (*"♥ Mochi purrs"*)
- **Tap the ground** and the nearest friend trots over
- **Drag the rain slider** from drizzle to downpour
- **Watch the fire** — it flares and spits embers at every quarter milestone of your session
- **Finish a session** — everyone sprints to the fire and parties

![Celebration](screenshots/readme-party.png)

## Quiet details

- The marshmallows over the fire sway as if held by invisible campers
- Shooting stars streak by every half-minute or so — make a wish
- Wind gusts slant the rain and bend the bare trees
- The tab title counts down (`09:55 · Study Watch`) so you can study in another tab
- Your timer, friends, rain level, and daily focus stats all survive a refresh

**Space** = start / pause · **R** = reset
